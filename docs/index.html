Ways around the game that players took and that the Advanced Map plugin for RuneLite has no transport for yet.

A row is one way: the thing used, where it stands, and where it leads. People counts the separate senders that reported it, a day at a time; Reports counts every report. A way many people report is most likely real; a way reported once may be a mistake.

What is collected, and what is not

Only players who turn on the plugin's "Report missing transports" setting send anything; it is off by default and warns before it turns on. A report is seven numbers and the plugin's version: whether an object or an NPC was used, its id, which of its menu options, its tile, the tile the player stood on, and the tile the player arrived at. Tiles are x, y and floor.

No name, no account id and nothing typed is sent. The receiver sees the sender's IP address, as any website does; it uses it only to make a mark that lasts one day, so one sender counts once a day for a way and cannot post more than 60 reports a day. The address is never stored, and the marks are deleted after two days. Names and menu options on this page are looked up from the game's data by id.

The rows are in data/reports.json, written every half hour when they change.
